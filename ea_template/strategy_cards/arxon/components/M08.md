## Source-derived mechanism
Daily zones describe price-location bands based on a prior daily range, not a complete entry signal.

## Proposed EA use — not a frozen trading rule
One location filter with an independent trigger; do not manufacture entries solely from zone colour.
No parent, instrument, timeframe, entry, exit, risk recipe or trade direction is selected. This is a component design card, not evidence of a complete standalone EA.

## Required semantic freeze
- daily calendar and range basis
- zone fractions/version
- boundary equality
- zone entry/exit and expiry

## Proposed deterministic outputs
`zone_bounds`, `location_state`, `source_day`. These are **proposed field names**, not an installed schema/API. Bind the final adapter to the existing data conventions before implementation.

## Causal timing and lifecycle
Record source bar/period, `extreme_or_origin_at`, `confirmed_at` and `first_action_at` separately where relevant. A plotted earlier pivot/zone is not an earlier observation. Freeze completed-bar or developing-bar policy, event rearm/expiry, restart behavior and unknown data handling before any consumer runs. Display-only output must not change historical trade decisions.
