## Executive summary

The current repository contains a substantial, tested Boss V2 chassis, but it is not yet an owner-easy product flow. Its strongest areas are the shared position, sizing, exit, risk, full-set, fingerprint, and build-evidence mechanisms. Its largest product gaps sit above and between those mechanisms: logical identity, the absent `xx-00` control artifact, complete effective-configuration projection, per-family applicability, and an end-to-end recipe/catalog view.

Classification count (36 capabilities): **IMPLEMENTED 16 · PARTIAL 15 · MISSING 2 · DRIFTED 2 · OBSOLETE 1**.

Interpretation rules for this map:

- `IMPLEMENTED` at chassis level does not mean enabled, applicable, or accepted for every Boss. Defaults and compile-time entry paths still control each EA.
- A source branch without a credible cage or owner-usable integration is not promoted to end-to-end implementation.
- A different `.set`, numeric center, symbol, timeframe, broker, or account profile is configuration/evidence identity, not automatically a logical child variant and not evidence of correlation or diversification.
- Recovery is same-direction losing-basket management; Hedge is opposite-direction exposure; Stack is ordinary position construction. They are not interchangeable.
- `PROG_PLUS` is additive (`firstLot + plus * level`); `PROG_LINEAR` is proportional (`firstLot * (1 + factor * level)`).

## Top remediation priorities

1. **P0 — Repair the identity model first.** Separate `FamilyID`, `LogicalVariantID`, hypothesis revision, Home, ParameterSet/config, build, and evidence snapshot. Preserve existing H01/package IDs through explicit aliases rather than rewriting accepted artifacts. Every catalog, scaffold, and reference artifact depends on this vocabulary.
2. **P0 — Define the `xx-00` reference contract after identity is stable.** Create the schema and one family instance only after the owner answers the unresolved Stack/Exit/SL/native-mechanic questions. It must remain a research control, not an optimized or LIVE preset.
3. **P0 — Resolve execution authority and exit ownership before broadening modules.** Specify same-tick Hedge/Recovery behavior and close the pyramid leg-0 TP conflict. Any implementation is core/risk behavior: use an owner-approved contract, negative tests, full template regression, and qualified different-family review.
4. **P1 — Create one canonical resolved-effective configuration projection.** It must emit Requested/Effective/Ignored/Locked/Reason, drive optimizer exposure and owner rendering, and remain distinct from the existing declared surface+constants fingerprint.
5. **P1 — Prove one real Boss through the complete owner recipe.** Join family/logical variant, Home, modules, full set, declared and resolved config identity, build receipt, same-lane evidence, status/blocker, and next action. Do this before calling the Factory owner-easy.
6. **P1 — Complete the scaffold as an orchestrated workflow.** Once priorities 1 and 4 stabilize, extend the current transactional generator through generated surfaces, identity artifacts, compile/cages/regression, and recipe registration without hiding manual strategy work.
7. **P2 — Add reusable News and Spread evidence layers.** Keep historical joins separate from runtime response changes; require real calendar and bid/ask/tick-spread provenance and report the full outcome deltas.
8. **P2 — Expand Recovery/Hedge/filter libraries only for direct consumers.** Preserve current working subsets; each new mechanism is one bounded child with explicit cap, owner, applicability, and falsification cage.

1. For `xx-00`, is baseline `StackConfirm` SignalValid, a specific Price Action mode, or family-specific?

5. What is the precise boundary between a one-mechanism logical child and a hypothesis/config revision, and how should existing H01 `VariantID` values be retained as aliases?
6. When Hedge and Recovery triggers are true on the same tick, must Hedge merely block new Recovery, cancel pending Recovery intent, or also unwind prior Recovery exposure?
7. Is Home identity only logical Symbol/TF with broker/account/parameter profile attached at run/evidence level, or should any of those fields be inside the durable Home contract?

Until those decisions are made, the corresponding remediation must remain schema/design work or `BLOCKED(<question>)`, not inferred runtime behavior.
