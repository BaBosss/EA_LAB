## Comparison contract

`parse_native_ledger.py` consumes a later actual tester journal and the expected Python ledger. It rejects:

- missing, duplicate or extra cases;
- an absent or duplicate completed sentinel;
- wrong fixture or source-graph hash;
- an unknown, placeholder or mismatched frozen runtime-contract ID;
- generated/unknown native origin;
- wrong variant, status, replay-return relation, direction, current-bar identity, setup/trigger time, geometry or proxy/profile identity;
- a malformed or extra native field.

Floating representations are compared as exact fixed-decimal strings at 12 decimal places. There is no range tolerance or `isclose` allowance. A difference at the twelfth decimal place fails, while ordinary Python/MQL binary64 display noise below that serialization precision cannot create a false mismatch. This precision is far below any frozen branch threshold or fixture price increment and cannot conceal a branch or geometry change.

A parser `PASS` is named `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE_NATIVE_PARITY_ONLY`. A material difference is `FAIL` and must not be repaired by mutating the accepted OFP components, reference, fixture or thresholds.
