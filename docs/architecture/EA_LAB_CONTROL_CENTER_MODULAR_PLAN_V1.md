## 6. Interface rules that make later merging small
CC00 defines versioned records for SourceObservation, GuardObservation, MarketState, EAObservation, ResearchArtifactRef, ResearchRequest, ModelPackageRef, Recommendation, CommandIntent and CommandReceipt. Extend existing report/SafeProjection/job records through explicit adapters; do not create parallel sources for the same fact.
Each observation carries source identity, schema/version, event/observation/available-at times where applicable, clock basis, quality state, evidence/config hash and freshness policy identity. Runtime observations and historical research records remain distinguishable. Freshness thresholds are domain-specific; the Monitor's daily-age policy must not be reused as trading-feed safety.
Guard records separate observed producer health, raw state, configured mode, requested policy, last-confirmed effective application and reason. Missing/stale/disabled/not-supported are different from ALLOW and BLOCK. Existing fail-open behavior is reported honestly; changing it is a separate consequential strategy/risk contract.
Command records bind authenticated actor, target device/installation/account/deployment/attach epoch, exact desired action, expected state/config/version, command ID, monotonic sequence, issue/expiry times and approval basis. Secrets and unmasked account identifiers stay out of mobile/public projections.
UI state is DRAFT -> VALIDATED -> AUTHORIZED -> SENT -> ACK/REJECT/EXPIRED/UNKNOWN. ACK means a documented applied transition, not broker fill or successful liquidation unless separately evidenced. Bulk requests retain per-EA outcomes; timeout is UNKNOWN, never silent success. Retry/idempotency must not duplicate action. Changed scope/config/target invalidates an old approval.
Distinguish starting a new basket, adding to an existing basket, placing new pending orders, managing existing pending orders, defensive hedging, and closing. BLOCK_NEW, NO_ADD, REDUCE and FLATTEN are not interchangeable. A NewsGuard block does not prove existing pending orders were cancelled. Restoring NORMAL or increasing an allowance can expand risk and requires its own valid authority.
The runtime safety objective remains local hard cages and owned exits functioning without UI/Python/network. A new external control lease must not suppress protective exits. Stale-state behavior and conflict precedence must be ratified per control profile rather than silently grafted onto the existing GV bridge.
A model package binds model hash, feature/schema/preprocessor hash, feature ordering, shapes/dtypes/opset, training window/data vintage, code/library identity, validation evidence and permitted consumers. P(BUY) is not automatically win probability. NO_ACTION/ABSTAIN is not FLATTEN. MQL5 inference does not imply online retraining.

## 8. Research integrity and AI scope
The clip supplies ideas: reuse the EA body, a separate trained model, features, build/prove/package flow, and operator tooling. It does not supply an independently validated algorithm, a ready data contract, guaranteed profitability, or permission to train/activate anything in EA_LAB.
For an AI gate, preregister the unit being predicted: entry opportunity, trade or EA-owned basket; label horizon, costs, exits and overlap handling. Keep source-emitted basket identity. Training labels may refer to later outcomes, but input features may only use information available at decision time. Fit normalizers/feature selection on training folds only.
Use chronological train/validation/walk-forward inside permitted development data; purge overlapping labels where required. Record all attempted models/variants, not just the winner. Freeze the model, gate threshold, baseline controls and acceptance before the final comparison. A frozen model tested on older BWD is a retrospective stress test, not evidence that the later-trained model was available then.
BWD/HOLDOUT are not training/search surfaces. No current data pulled today may be relabeled as historical point-in-time knowledge. Preserve historical vintages and actual publication availability. MQL5 economic-calendar timestamps use broker trade-server time; conversion must use an explicit broker-clock/DST contract, not the user's Bangkok timezone.
Attribution is not a causal counterfactual: deleting trades that coincide with news cannot reconstruct the equity path of a filtered grid/basket system. “Avoided loss/missed profit” needs a valid paired rerun or a qualified causal replay; otherwise label it descriptive affected-trade/basket analysis.
Numerical EA evidence remains Model1 minimum. Candidate requires frozen Model4 MAIN+BWD on one authorized installation lineage, no retune, and the remaining canonical gates. Python model scores, shadow decisions and UI screenshots do not substitute for MT5 research evidence. Do not invent KINT/grade/sample thresholds.
Guard testing needs positive firing plus a base control and negative cases; zero firings is untested, not safety PASS. Tests must distinguish model failure, data failure, harness failure and strategy evidence. Optional AI cannot override valid hard guards, increase lot, remove protection or open an unrelated strategy.
New proposals overlapping QI-2+ must be classified by CT and remain blocked until their existing owner boundary is satisfied. Do not relabel a parked Quant Intelligence project as an approved Control Center module.
