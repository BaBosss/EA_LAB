**Conclusion: H1 is genuinely optimal** — the hardcoding was correct. Lower TFs trade far more but the
breakout edge disappears (PF<1); H4 is too sparse. The ~13 trades/yr thinness at H1 is inherent to the
strategy, NOT fixable by dropping TF. BRK-XAU stays H1 (demo #6). Edge is TF-specific — same pattern as
RSI-MR being EURUSD-specific and Zeus being gold-specific: each edge has one home (instrument or scale).
The TFvar source is kept for reference; the H1 build remains the deploy artifact.
