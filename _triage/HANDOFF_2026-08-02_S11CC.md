## The one defect worth carrying forward

**A prohibition can disarm the check that enforces it, and the disarming looks like the design
working.**

The rule is "Telegram must not be able to read the full snapshot". The scan that proves a projection
carries no account number works partly by looking for **literals taken from the snapshot** — which
is the layer that cannot false-positive on a timestamp, and is therefore the layer that matters. The
sender has no snapshot. So on the sender's side that layer is **empty**, and case `SB03` released a
document with a planted account number three levels down in a field nobody had blacklisted.

The fix that would have been wrong: add `source_account` to the forbidden-key list. It makes the
fixture green and the class of defect survives untouched, because **a blacklist of field names has
no end**. The fix that holds: check the **SHAPE** — an allowlist, **read from `schemas.json`** so it
cannot drift from the contract — and refuse an undeclared field *for being undeclared*.

Generalised: **when a boundary removes an input from one side, ask which of that side's checks were
silently reading it.** The check does not report that it lost its input; it reports success.
