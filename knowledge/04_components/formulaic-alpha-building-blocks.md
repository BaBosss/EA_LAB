# Formulaic alphas are reusable building-block patterns, not copy-paste EAs
`RC-SSRN2701346-001` shows how short-horizon alphas can be expressed as combinations of price, return, volume, VWAP, ranking, decay and industry-neutralization operators. The source mixes mean-reversion and momentum at the building-block level.

## Second Brain representation
When extracting an alpha idea, separate:
- raw observable inputs;
- transformation/operator;
- cross-sectional versus time-series semantics;
- delay / information-availability timing;
- sign and mechanism hypothesis;
- holding horizon;
- neutralization or portfolio requirement.

Do not transplant a formula if the target market lacks the same data timing, cross-sectional universe or execution assumptions.
