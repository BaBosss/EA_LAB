# RSI Role Depends on Strategy Semantics

Authority: `RESEARCH_ONLY`.

## Source-backed observation

`RC-SSRN3412429-001` shows a tested use of 14-day RSI in which sustained values above 40 encode bullish trend consistency and repeated moves above 70 encode strong upside momentum. In that source, `RSI > 70` is not automatically a reversal/sell condition.

The same indicator can therefore represent different hypotheses:

- reversal / mean reversion: an extreme may precede a retracement;
- trend consistency: RSI holding above a floor can describe persistent direction;
- momentum continuation: repeated high RSI can identify leadership/strength.

## Required research discipline

Do not attach a universal semantic meaning to an indicator threshold. Every strategy hypothesis should name the role the indicator is intended to play, the market/timeframe evidence supporting that role, and the falsification test.
