# Cross-Crypto Volatility Commonality

Authority: `RESEARCH_ONLY`.

## Source-backed observation

`RC-SSRN5048674-001` studies twenty liquid cryptocurrency pairs and reports that one-day-ahead intraday realized-volatility forecasts improve when cross-asset/commonality information is introduced through clustered, cross-asset or pooled training schemes.

The source is about volatility state and volatility forecasting. Its economic application uses cryptocurrency options; it is not evidence that cross-crypto features directly predict BTC spot direction.

## Candidate feature families for future research

- BTC realized-volatility lags;
- peer-crypto realized-volatility lags;
- cluster/common volatility factors;
- network/spillover features;
- regime-specific forecast error.
