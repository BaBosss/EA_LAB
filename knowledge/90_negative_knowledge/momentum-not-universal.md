# Momentum Is Not Universal Across Markets

> RESEARCH_ONLY contradictory evidence. This is **not** QI Negative Experiment Memory and does not create an EA/experiment verdict.

## Positive source evidence

- `RC-SSRN3412429-001` reports that selected RSI range/momentum conditions in historical S&P 500 constituents can identify persistent trend/momentum behavior.
- `RC-SSRN1585517-001` reports positive relative-strength rotation results across long U.S.-sector and global-asset samples.

## Contradictory source evidence

`RC-SSRN2269045-001` finds very little abnormal risk-adjusted momentum performance over its full Irish-equity sample after using bootstrap inference to address highly non-normal and serially dependent returns. Some high-growth subperiod configurations are positive, but the authors describe them as conditional and unreliable as a general rule.

## EA_LAB_INFERENCE
