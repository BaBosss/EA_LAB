# Inflation Regime Research

Authority: `RESEARCH_ONLY`.

## Source-defined regime

`RC-SSRN3813202-001` / `SRC-GDRIVE-SSRN3813202` defines inflationary regimes as periods when headline year-over-year inflation is accelerating and reaches at least 5%. The study analyzes these as prolonged macro episodes rather than next-bar trading signals.

## Reported historical behavior

Across the source's U.S./U.K./Japan history, traditional equities and nominal bonds perform poorly in real terms during high-and-rising inflation episodes. Commodities are stronger in aggregate, and dynamic time-series trend following is reported as comparatively reliable protection during important inflation shocks.

## EA_LAB research use

Inflation can be retained as a contextual regime label for cross-paper synthesis. It does not become an EA entry rule, NewsGuard feed, MacroGate runtime input, or sizing/risk control through this note.
