# Crypto pairs: cointegration, copulas, costs and relationship selection

Primary reference: Tadi & Witzany, *Financial Innovation* 11:40 (2025), DOI:10.1186/s40854-024-00702-7; arXiv:2305.06961.

## SOURCE_CLAIM

The study develops a cryptocurrency pairs-trading framework combining cointegration tests and copulas. The published version uses twenty Binance USDT-margined futures coins, sets BTCUSDT as a reference asset, applies linear and nonlinear cointegration tests, ranks cointegrated candidates using Kendall's Tau, selects top correlated assets, updates the selection weekly, and derives trading signals from copula conditional probabilities of spread processes.

The source explicitly includes transaction fees in its reported calculations and compares its approach with conventional cointegration and copula pairs methods. The authors report stronger profitability/risk-adjusted results for their proposed method in the tested historical sample.

## Method / context

- Market: cryptocurrency USDT-margined futures on Binance.
- Universe: 20 coins; BTCUSDT is the source-study reference asset.
- Sampling examined: hourly and 5-minute data.
- Formation/selection: cointegration + correlation ranking; weekly updates.
- Signal family: copula conditional probability / mispricing on spread processes.
- Costs: transaction fees included by the study.

## Result

The paper supports three useful research points:
1. pair selection and relationship testing are first-class strategy mechanics;
2. nonlinear dependence can be modeled after a deterministic relationship baseline exists;
3. transaction costs materially belong in the strategy definition and comparison.

## Limitations / caveats

- Exchange, contract, fee, liquidity and funding conditions are source-specific.
- BTC-as-reference is a study design choice, not an EA_LAB default.
- Historical pair relationships can break.
- Higher-frequency gross performance can be misleading if costs/slippage/funding are not modeled consistently.
- The source's parameter thresholds and best-performing configuration are not imported.

## EA_LAB_INFERENCE

EA_LAB should begin a stat-arb family with a **deterministic cost-aware cointegration baseline**, not with the full copula/ML stack.

The baseline must freeze universe, pair selection, hedge/spread semantics, divergence/convergence rule, relationship-failure rule and all material costs before seeing outcome results. Copula dependence becomes a later one-change child only if the baseline shows a real pulse.
