# 151 Trading Strategies — EA_LAB Mechanism Library Seed

## SOURCE_CLAIM

The tracked EA_LAB catalog describes Kakushadze & Serur's *151 Trading Strategies* as a broad strategy reference with 550+ formulas, source-code examples for out-of-sample backtesting, and roughly 2,000 references. The EA_LAB extraction identifies MT5-relevant mechanisms including moving-average trend rules, pivot support/resistance, Donchian channels, IBS mean reversion, OU-style mean reversion, volatility-scaled trend following, and `tanh(R/kappa)` smoothing.

## Method / context

This card is migrated from an already-tracked EA_LAB derived catalog. The upstream paper was **not re-read** in this milestone. Many catalog entries are cross-sectional, multi-asset, or require data not native to retail MT5.

## Result

The source is useful as a mechanism encyclopedia and idea generator. It is not evidence that any individual mechanism is profitable on EA_LAB target symbols or timeframes.

## Limitations / caveats

- Derived-catalog evidence depth; consult upstream full text before relying on fine mathematical details.
- Many strategies were designed for asset classes or data feeds different from XAU/FX/retail CFDs.
- No EA_LAB backtest, OOS, robustness, or forward verdict is created by this card.
## EA_LAB_INFERENCE

Use the source as a reusable mechanism library. A candidate mechanism becomes only a `TESTABLE_HYPOTHESIS` after target-market transfer assumptions, costs, regime expectations, and validation needs are stated explicitly.
