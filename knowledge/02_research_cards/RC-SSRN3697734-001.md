# Optimized indicator parameters can beat defaults in-sample without proving a portable edge
## SOURCE_CLAIM
Mahajan and Inumula test standard versus optimized MACD/RSI rules on 30 continuously traded Indian NSE stocks across five sectors from April 2012 to March 2015. Their reported results say buy-and-hold outperformed the standard indicator rules, while optimized MACD/RSI variants outperformed both standard rules and buy-and-hold in their study.
## Method / context
- Market: Indian equities / NSE
- Period: April 2012–March 2015
- Tools: MetaStock simulation and Excel; paired t-tests
- Costs: transaction cost assumption of Rs 50 per trade
## Limitations / caveats
The study is a short, single-market historical sample and explicitly searches optimized indicator settings. The source does not establish that the selected settings remain effective out of sample, across instruments, or after a broader multiple-testing correction.
## EA_LAB_INFERENCE
Treat optimized MACD/RSI results as parameter-hypothesis evidence only. Any reuse must preserve the full search history and pass independent OOS/robustness gates; this card should be read with `RC-SSRN2474755-001` rather than as permission to optimize until a winner appears.
