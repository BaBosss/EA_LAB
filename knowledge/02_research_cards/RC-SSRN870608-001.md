# Market open/close boundaries can change information flow and trading intensity
## SOURCE_CLAIM
Boulatov and Livdan model strategic informed trading when markets close periodically and private information accumulates during closures. Their equilibrium produces time-varying trading intensity and a U-shaped volume pattern during open periods as traders respond to newly accumulated information and anticipated future closures.
## Method / context
- Theoretical dynamic-auction model with informed traders, market maker and noise traders
- Periodic predictable market closures
- Informed traders receive private signals while the market is closed
- Strategic behavior makes the trading pattern aperiodic even with periodic closures
## Result

The model produces time-varying, approximately U-shaped trading intensity around open periods and shows that periodic market closures can alter information asymmetry and the strategic timing of informed trading. These are model results, not a retail entry-signal backtest.

## Limitations / caveats
This is a theoretical microstructure model, not a tested retail trading strategy. It assumes specific information and agent structures and does not provide a direct MT5 entry rule or expected edge around sessions.
## EA_LAB_INFERENCE
Session/open/close boundaries deserve explicit regime/context labels when a strategy is sensitive to liquidity or information shocks. The source supports testing session effects, not hard-coding a profitable session rule.
