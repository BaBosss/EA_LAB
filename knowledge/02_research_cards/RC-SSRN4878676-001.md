# Strategy development is an iterative contract, not just indicator selection
## SOURCE_CLAIM
Long and Bhattacharyya develop intraday mean-reversion strategies on QuantConnect using RSI, MACD and Bollinger Bands while explicitly considering transaction fees and broker-deployable implementation. They adopt Pardo's strategy-development sequence and organize refinement around entry/exit, risk management and position sizing.
## Method / context
- Market focus: NYSE / intraday mean reversion
- Platform: QuantConnect, with practical broker implementation in view
- Research loop: formulation, definite rules, preliminary test, optimization, walk-forward, real-time evaluation and improvement
- Auxiliary ideas include stop-loss/trailing-stop choices and Kelly-style sizing
## Limitations / caveats
This is a practical strategy-building study, not evidence that RSI/MACD/Bollinger or Kelly sizing is universally profitable. Hyperparameter searches and iterative refinement can themselves create selection bias unless the experiment history is controlled.
## EA_LAB_INFERENCE
The durable value is the workflow: mechanism hypothesis → exact rules → realistic costs → bounded optimization → walk-forward/real-time evaluation. Factory remains the verifier; this source does not authorize a candidate, sizing rule, deployment or risk change.
