# Cross-crypto realized-volatility commonality can improve one-day-ahead volatility forecasts

## SOURCE_CLAIM

Djanga, Cucuringu and Zhang study intraday realized-volatility commonality across twenty liquid USDT cryptocurrency pairs. They forecast one-day-ahead intraday realized volatility using single-asset, clustered, cross-asset and pooled/universal training schemes so that commonality enters progressively.

The source reports that models using cross-crypto commonality outperform models that do not explicitly account for it across the studied regimes. Forecasts are evaluated out of sample using walk-forward testing and are also used for spillover/network analysis.

The paper further applies the volatility forecasts to cryptocurrency-option trading strategies and reports better P&L/risk-adjusted outcomes than a benchmark GARCH-based signal in its tested setup.

## Method / context

- Market / asset: 20 liquid Binance USDT cryptocurrency pairs; option-chain application for economic testing
- Timeframe / horizon: 30-minute realized volatility resampled at 5 minutes; forecast 1D-ahead intraday RV
- Sample period: January 2021 through June 2023
- Method: ML/deep-learning models under four training schemes; cross-asset commonality; walk-forward evaluation; spillover network analysis
- Comparison / benchmark: non-commonality models and GARCH-based forecast/trading benchmark
## Result

Within the source's crypto universe, volatility was not purely asset-local: cross-asset information improved realized-volatility forecasting and produced economically useful signals in the authors' option-trading experiment.

## Limitations / caveats

This is a volatility-forecasting result, not direct evidence for spot BTC directional entry. The trading application uses cryptocurrency options and market/data infrastructure unlike a retail MT5 BTC CFD. The sample is 2021-2023 and should not be assumed invariant across later crypto regimes.

## EA_LAB_INFERENCE

BTC volatility context may benefit from a bounded cross-crypto sidecar using other liquid crypto assets rather than BTC-only features. The first consumer should be a forecasting/ablation hypothesis; it must not be promoted into entry, sizing or risk logic without separate validation and authority.
