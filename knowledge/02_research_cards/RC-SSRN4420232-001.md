# Arbitrage is a family label that hides very different instruments and risks
## SOURCE_CLAIM
Burgess provides an introductory taxonomy of five arbitrage families: volatility arbitrage neutral, volatility arbitrage long-biased, structured-product arbitrage, capital-structure arbitrage and credit arbitrage. The paper illustrates each with simplified examples and references.
## Method / context
- Educational overview rather than empirical backtest
- Instruments span options, structured products, equity, bonds and credit derivatives
- Common framing is relative mispricing with controlled directional exposure
## Result

The source's outcome is a five-family arbitrage taxonomy with worked conceptual examples and references. It does not report an empirical profitability test, so no performance result is promoted from this source.

## Limitations / caveats
The source does not supply empirical profitability evidence, a unified risk model, or retail implementation validation. Several examples require instruments and market access outside the current MT5 EA universe.
## EA_LAB_INFERENCE
Use this source only to expand the strategy-family ontology and to reject the false assumption that all arbitrage strategies are equivalent. Any arbitrage hypothesis must specify instrument pair, pricing relation, hedge, execution and convergence mechanism.
