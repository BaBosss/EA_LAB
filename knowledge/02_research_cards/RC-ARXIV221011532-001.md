# DNN-ForwardTesting adds a predicted-future strategy-selection layer

Primary reference: Letteri, Della Penna, De Gasperis & Dyoub, arXiv:2210.11532 (2022).

## SOURCE_CLAIM

The paper proposes `DNN-forwardtesting`: instead of selecting a technical strategy only from historical backtest performance, it trains a deep feed-forward neural network on historical market data, forecasts the next 30 open-market days, tests candidate technical indicators on those predicted future prices, and uses the selected indicator for real future trading.

The study begins with ten securities, groups/filters assets using a volatility analysis, trains the DNN on a reduced set, and reports that this forward-selection approach improves Expectancy, Sharpe, Sortino and Calmar ratios relative to the strategy selected through traditional backtesting in its experiments.

## Method / context

- Universe examined: ten securities before source-specific filtering.
- Forecast horizon: next 30 open-market days.
- Forecast model: deep feed-forward neural network.
- Strategy choice: technical indicator selected on the model-predicted future.

## Result

The source demonstrates a research design where a learned future path becomes a strategy-selection surface. It reports positive comparative results in its study.

## Limitations / caveats

- The predicted future is model output, not independent future evidence.
- Forecast-model error can propagate into strategy-selection error.
- Model choice, asset filtering, technical-indicator candidate set and historical training data all add degrees of freedom.
- The reported result does not establish replacement of real chronological OOS/HOLDOUT.
- Transfer to MT5 FX/XAU/BTC is untested.

## EA_LAB_INFERENCE

Treat this as **scenario/selection research**, not as a validation authority.

The failure chain is:
`forecast-model error -> synthetic-future error -> strategy-selection error`.

A future experiment would need to compare model-generated scenarios against real chronological windows under strict separation. Until then, DNN-forwardtesting stays below the normal EA_LAB BWD/OOS/HOLDOUT evidence hierarchy.
