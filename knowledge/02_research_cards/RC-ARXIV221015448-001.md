# Neural-augmented Kalman/Bollinger pairs trading is an advanced child, not a baseline

Primary reference: Milstein, Deng, Revach, Morgenstern & Shlezinger, arXiv:2210.15448 (2022).

## SOURCE_CLAIM

The paper starts from a common pairs-trading representation: a linear state-space relationship between two assets, estimated with a Kalman Filter and converted into trading signals through Bollinger-Band-style logic.

It argues that the assumed state-space model is approximate and can be mismatched. The proposed method, KBPT, uses an extended state-space model with partial cointegration and augments the Kalman-filter/Bollinger pipeline with a KalmanNet neural architecture.

Training is two-stage: first tune tracking independently of the trading objective, then adapt the financial-indicator tracking to maximize revenue while approximating the Bollinger decision mapping differentiably. The authors report improved revenue versus model-based and data-driven benchmarks across the tested assets.

## Method / context

- Mechanism: dynamic pair relationship / state-space estimation.
- Baseline: Kalman Filter + Bollinger Bands.
- Extension: neural KalmanNet augmentation under partial cointegration.
- Training: tracking stage followed by trading-objective adaptation.

## Result

The source supports neural augmentation as one possible response to model mismatch in a dynamic pairs relationship.

## Limitations / caveats

- Added model flexibility increases degrees of freedom and model risk.
- Revenue improvement in the source study is not proof of transfer to EA_LAB assets/broker execution.
- A neural hedge/spread estimator can obscure whether any edge exists in the underlying deterministic relationship.
- This paper does not justify skipping cost, structural-break, baseline or chronological validation work.

## EA_LAB_INFERENCE

Do not start the stat-arb family here.

First establish a deterministic cost-aware baseline. If the baseline has a reproducible pulse but shows evidence that fixed/deterministic relationship estimation is the limiting mechanism, a Kalman dynamic-hedge child may be tested. Neural augmentation would be a still-later child with one logical change and its own overfit controls.
