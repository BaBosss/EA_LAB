# A large alpha library is a mechanism catalog, not a list of production strategies
## SOURCE_CLAIM
Kakushadze presents explicit formulas for 101 quantitative trading alphas, mostly built from price/volume inputs with some industry-neutralization and fundamental information. The paper reports average holding periods of roughly 0.6–6.4 days and low average pair-wise alpha correlation of 15.9% in the proprietary sample.
## Method / context
- Alpha building blocks include both mean-reversion and momentum ideas
- Several formulas use open/high/low/close, volume and VWAP transforms
- Source reports alpha returns strongly related to volatility and no significant direct dependence on turnover in its sample
- Empirical properties rely on proprietary data supplied with permission
## Limitations / caveats
The formulas are examples from a specific institutional equity-alpha context; the paper does not establish that copying them to another market, latency model or retail execution stack preserves edge. Low correlation among source alphas is not proof of low correlation among EA_LAB implementations.
## EA_LAB_INFERENCE
Use the paper to expand the mechanism/component vocabulary and generate bounded hypotheses. Each adopted building block still requires independent semantics, data availability, execution realism and validation in EA_LAB.
