# Strategy Blueprint Boundary

This directory stores research synthesis and pointers only. It must not become a second strategy registry.

Canonical strategy identity remains `{ea_id, strategy_revision}` and StrategyRecordV1 remains additive to `factory/strategy_catalog.json` under the frozen QI-1 decision.

Allowed here:

- non-authoritative blueprint notes derived from research;
- links to canonical strategy IDs when a research hypothesis is later adopted;
- component/mechanism/regime relationships used to explain a hypothesis.

Forbidden here:

- a competing `ea_id` or strategy identity system;
- lifecycle/verdict authority;
- production parameter defaults;
- automatic Factory, deployment, or trading actions.
