# Cross-Sectional Relative Strength

## Thesis

Rank a defined universe by trailing performance and hold the strongest subset, rather than asking whether one instrument is individually above or below its own trend reference.

## Evidence

`RC-SSRN1585517-001` reports positive long-horizon results for monthly U.S.-sector and global-asset rotation across several ranking windows. The same source also reports substantial residual volatility/drawdown and material implementation frictions.

`RC-SSRN2269045-001` is contradictory evidence: Irish equity winner-minus-loser momentum showed little abnormal risk-adjusted return over the full sample once non-normality and serial dependence were treated more carefully.

## Transfer boundary

Relative strength is not interchangeable with single-asset time-series trend. Any future EA_LAB hypothesis must specify ranking universe, lookback, top/bottom selection, rebalance cadence, turnover, tradability and cost assumptions.

The mixed source record means momentum/relative-strength evidence is market- and horizon-conditional. No portfolio automation, risk/default change, Factory acceptance or trading authority is created here.
