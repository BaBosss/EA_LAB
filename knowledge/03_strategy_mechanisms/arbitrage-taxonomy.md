# Arbitrage requires an explicit convergence relation
`RC-SSRN4420232-001` supplies a broad taxonomy spanning volatility, structured-product, capital-structure and credit arbitrage. `RC-SSRN2147012-001` provides a narrower empirical/theoretical lens for statistical arbitrage and pairs/cointegration under changing market microstructure.

## Required mechanism fields
- instruments or legs;
- mispricing / relative-value relation;
- hedge or neutrality definition;
- convergence/exit condition;
- funding, borrow, option or derivative requirements;
- execution synchronization and cost assumptions;
- structural-break refusal condition.

The label `arbitrage` is not evidence of low risk or guaranteed convergence. Strategies requiring unavailable instruments remain research references, not EA candidates.
